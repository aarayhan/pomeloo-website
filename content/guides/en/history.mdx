Tray icon → **History…** lists your recent jobs, newest first.

<Screenshot src="history" alt="The History window with Show, Undo and Again buttons" />

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show** | Selects the results in Explorer |
| **Undo** | Moves the results to the Recycle Bin; renames are reversed |
| **Again** | Runs the same job on the same files, with the same settings |

The notch's **Recent** tab has the latest results with Undo too, one hover away.

<Note>
  Undo never deletes your originals: PomeloO never changed them in the first place. Passwords (from Add Password)
  are never stored in History.
</Note>
