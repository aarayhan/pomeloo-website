## Update

PomeloO updates itself. Once a day it checks GitHub; when a new version is out it downloads it, checks its SHA-256,
and installs it while you're not using the PC (nothing running, no window open), then starts again and says so in
the notch. Your settings, recipes and history are kept. See [what's new](../../changelog/).

- To update right away: tray icon → **Check for updates…**
- To turn it off: Settings → **Update automatically**. Then update by downloading the newest installer from the
  [download page](../../download/) and running it.
- The portable zip doesn't update itself; PomeloO tells you when a new version is out.

## Uninstall

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps">Or search for "Add or remove programs".</Step>
  <Step title="Find PomeloO → ⋯ → Uninstall">It closes the app first.</Step>
</Steps>

This also removes the right-click menu, the start-with-Windows entry and the private LibreOffice copy (if one was
downloaded). Your settings in `%APPDATA%\PomeloO` stay, in case you come back; delete that folder to remove them too.

<Note>
  Using the portable zip? Tray → **Remove from Explorer menu**, turn off **Start with Windows** in Settings, quit,
  then delete the folder.
</Note>
