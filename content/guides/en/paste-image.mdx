---
title: Paste an image as a file
description: Copy any image, press Ctrl+V in a folder, and it's saved there as a PNG.
group: everyday
order: 8
---

Windows can't paste a copied picture into a folder. With PomeloO it can.

<Demo name="paste" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy an image">
    Right-click an image in the browser → **Copy image**, take a screenshot with <Keys k="Win+Shift+S" />, or copy
    from any app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a folder in Explorer, or click the desktop">Wherever you want the file.</Step>
  <Step title="Press Ctrl+V">
    The image is saved there as `Pasted image 2026-10-23 14.05.09.png` (the date and time of the paste) and selected,
    ready to rename.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This only kicks in when the clipboard holds an image and no files, so pasting copied files works just like
  before. Turn it off in Settings → **Paste images as files**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Just took a screenshot? The notch also offers to save it, read its text, annotate it or send it to your phone.
</Tip>
