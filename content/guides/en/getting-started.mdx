PomeloO is a small app that lives in the system tray. There's no main window to open: you use it straight
from Explorer, while you drag files.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the installer">
    Get **PomeloO-Setup-x.y.z.exe** from the [download page](../../download/). It needs Windows 10 (1809 or newer) or
    Windows 11, 64-bit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    No admin rights are needed. PomeloO installs for your user only, adds a Start menu entry and, if you leave
    the box ticked, the Explorer right-click menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="&ldquo;Windows protected your PC&rdquo;? Click More info → Run anyway">
    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. The source code and the build
    that made it are public on GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the orange icon in the tray">
    PomeloO starts right away and from now on with Windows. Click the **^** arrow next to the clock if you
    don't see the icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On its first start PomeloO compresses its own folder with Windows' built-in file compression (about 690 MB
  → 240 MB on disk). It does this quietly in the background, two minutes after launch.
</Note>

## Your first conversion

<Demo name="wheel" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a folder with a photo in it">For example your Pictures or Downloads folder.</Step>
  <Step title="Start dragging the photo">Press and hold the left mouse button on it and move a little.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Shift while you're still holding the mouse button">
    A wheel of formats appears under the cursor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move onto a format and let go">
    The converted copy appears next to the original, named like `photo.png`. The original is never changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Changed your mind? Let go anywhere outside the wheel, or press <Keys k="Esc" />. Nothing happens to the file.
</Tip>

## The tray icon

Right-click the orange icon for **Receive from phone**, **Recipes**, **History** and **Settings**. Everything
PomeloO does can be tuned in Settings: the keys, the shortcuts, the notch and more.
