Drag a photo and tap <Keys k="Ctrl" /> to open its tools. Each tool has a live preview; press the orange button to
save the result as a new file next to the original.

## Edit Photo

<Screenshot src="tool-editImage" alt="The Edit Photo window with sliders and a preview" />

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, temperature, tint, clarity, dehaze, sharpness, noise
reduction, grain and vignette, plus rotate, flip and black & white. Double-click a slider to reset it.

## Annotate

<Screenshot src="tool-annotateImage" alt="The Annotate window" />

Pen, highlighter, arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, numbered callouts, text, a magnifier, and other images on top.
Great for explaining a screenshot.

## Add Background

<Screenshot src="tool-frameImage" alt="The Add Background window" />

Put the picture on a solid colour, a gradient, another image or a blurred copy of itself. Choose the aspect
ratio, spacing, rounded corners and shadow: perfect for social posts and slides.

## Crop

<Screenshot src="tool-cropImage" alt="The Crop window" />

Drag a region (lock it to an aspect ratio and move it around) or type exact pixel dimensions.

## Redact

<Screenshot src="tool-redactImage" alt="The Redact window" />

Hide faces, names or numbers. Add regions by hand, or let PomeloO find faces and text automatically (with Windows'
built-in detectors, on your PC). Pick pixelate, blur or a solid colour.

## More

- **Read QR Code** copies what's in a QR code in the photo.
- **Copy Text (OCR)** and **Searchable PDF**: see [Copy text from the screen](../screen-text/).
- **Metadata** shows camera, date and location info; edit it or strip it all before you share.

## Several photos

Select several photos and drag them together for **Create PDF** (one page per photo) and **Create Collage**.
