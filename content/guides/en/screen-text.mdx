Text in a screenshot, a paused video, a photo of a whiteboard or a locked PDF: if you can see it, you can copy it.

<Demo name="ocr" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Win+Ctrl+T">
    The screen dims slightly and the cursor becomes a crosshair. You can also click **Copy text from screen** in the notch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag a box over the text">Let go and PomeloO reads it with Windows' own text recognition.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste it anywhere">The text is on the clipboard. A small notice tells you how many words were copied.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="ocr-snip" alt="Dragging a box over text on the screen" />

<Tip>
  A QR code in the box? Only its contents (the link) are copied, not the text around it. And if you just copied an
  image, <Keys k="Win+Ctrl+T" /> reads
  that image directly, without dragging.
</Tip>

## For files: Copy text and Searchable PDF

For whole files, use the tools wheel (<Keys k="Ctrl" /> while dragging):

- **Copy Text (OCR)** on a photo or PDF puts all its text on the clipboard. PDF pages that already have real text
  are used as they are; scanned pages are read.
- **Searchable PDF** adds an invisible text layer over scans and photos, so you can search, select and copy in any
  PDF reader.

<Note>
  PomeloO reads the languages installed in Windows. To add one: Settings → Time & language → Language & region →
  Add a language. Press <Keys k="Esc" /> to cancel a selection. The shortcut can be changed in Settings → Keyboard.
</Note>
