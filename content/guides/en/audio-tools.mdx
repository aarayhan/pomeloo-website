Drag an audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, AIFF, WMA) and tap <Keys k="Ctrl" />. Every audio tool has
**Original** and **Result** buttons so you can hear the difference before you save.

## Normalize Volume

<Screenshot src="tool-normalizeAudio" alt="The Normalize Volume window" />

Makes quiet recordings louder and loud ones calmer, to a standard loudness (two-pass EBU R128). Set the loudness,
range and true-peak targets, or keep the defaults. The input and output levels are shown.

## More tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Trim Audio** | In/Out marks on a player; optionally remove silences |
| **Bleep Audio** | Mark ranges to beep or mute, like a swear word or a name |
| **Convert Audio Channels** | Mono or stereo, with separate left and right volume |
| **Audio Visualizer** | Turns audio into a video: animated waveform, spectrum or a still image; landscape, portrait or square |
| **Compress** | Smaller files; WAV and AIFF become lossless FLAC |
| **Metadata** | Title, artist, album and the rest; edit or strip |

<Tip>
  Recorded a talk or a voice note? **Normalize** first, then **Trim** the start and end. A [recipe](../recipes/) can do
  both in one go.
</Tip>
