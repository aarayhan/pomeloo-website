title: Slides (PPT and PPTX)
description: How PomeloO turns presentations into PDF or images at full fidelity.
group: setup
order: 18
---

Presentations are hard to draw correctly, so PomeloO lets a real Office engine do it: an installed **LibreOffice**,
**WPS Office** or **PowerPoint**, whichever you have.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag a PPT or PPTX and tap Shift">Choose **PDF**, **JPG** or **PNG** (one image per slide).</Step>
  <Step title="No Office suite installed?">
    The first time, PomeloO downloads a private copy of LibreOffice (about 375 MB, from The Document Foundation,
    checksum-verified). No admin rights are needed, and it doesn't touch the rest of your PC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next time it's instant">The engine is kept for later conversions.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The private copy lives in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PomeloO\libreoffice` and is removed when you uninstall PomeloO. This
  download is one of the few times PomeloO uses the internet. See [Privacy](../../privacy/).
</Note>
