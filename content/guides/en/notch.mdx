The notch is a small black pill at the top centre of your screen. It stays out of the way until you need it.

<Screenshot src="notch-pill-shelf" alt="The notch as a small pill" caption="Resting: just a pill. It tells you how many files are on the shelf." />

<Demo name="notch" />

## Open the panel

Hover the pill for a moment, or click it. The panel slides down with five tabs and a **Copy text from screen**
button. Move the mouse away and it folds back up.

### Shelf

<Screenshot src="notch-shelf" alt="The Shelf tab with four files" />

A place to park files while you work: drop files on the panel to keep them handy. They stay on the shelf after a
restart.

- **Drag a file out** to any app, folder or chat to use it there.
- **Shift+drag** a file out to drop it converted: PNG → JPG, DOCX → PDF, MKV → MP4…
- **Hover** a file for a preview.
- **Clear** empties the shelf (your files themselves are not touched).

### Jobs

<Screenshot src="notch-jobs" alt="The Jobs tab with a running compression" />

Every conversion and tool shows its progress here, with **Cancel**. When it's done you see how much space was
saved and a **Show** button that selects the result in Explorer.

### Clipboard

<Screenshot src="notch-clipboard" alt="The Clipboard tab" />

The last six things you copied (text and images), ready to copy again or drag out. It lives in memory only,
is never saved to disk, and skips anything a password manager marks as private.

### Recent

<Screenshot src="notch-recent" alt="The Recent tab" />

Your latest results, ready to drag out, each with **Undo**.

### Phone

The QR code to send files to your phone and receive files from it, right in the notch. See
[Send and receive with your phone](../phone/).

## Notices and ideas

Done messages, size saved and files arriving from the phone appear in the notch instead of Windows balloons.

<Screenshot src="notch-toast" alt="A done notice in the notch" />

When a new file lands in Downloads, the notch suggests a next step, like **→ JPG** for a HEIC photo. A copied
screenshot can be saved as a file, read (OCR), annotated or sent to the phone in one click.

<Screenshot src="notch-suggest" alt="An idea for a downloaded file" />

## It knows when to hide

- It steps aside for full-screen apps, games and presentations.
- It never appears in screenshots or screen sharing.
- On battery saver it skips its animations.

Change its position (left, centre, right), screen, and which tabs it shows in Settings → **Notch**. Next:
[drop files on the notch](../notch-drop/).
