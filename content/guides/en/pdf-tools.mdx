Drag a PDF and tap <Keys k="Ctrl" />. To turn a PDF into Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images or EPUB, tap
<Keys k="Shift" /> instead.

<Screenshot src="wheel-tools-pdf" alt="The tools wheel for a PDF" />

## Merge PDFs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select all the PDFs">Ctrl+click them in Explorer.</Step>
  <Step title="Drag them together and tap Ctrl">Choose **Merge PDFs**.</Step>
  <Step title="Put them in order, then Merge">One combined PDF is saved next to the first file.</Step>
</Steps>

## Organize PDF

<Screenshot src="tool-organizePDF" alt="The Organize PDF window with page thumbnails" />

Reorder, rotate, duplicate and delete pages, with thumbnails. **Split PDF** cuts a PDF into several files.

## Password

<Screenshot src="tool-protectPDF" alt="The Add Password window" />

**Add Password** locks the PDF with AES-256. **Remove Password** makes an unlocked copy (you need the current
password). Passwords are never stored, not even in History.

## Watermark and page numbers

<Screenshot src="tool-watermarkPDF" alt="The Add Watermark window" />

**Add Watermark**: your text, size, opacity, angle and colour on every page.

<Screenshot src="tool-numberPDF" alt="The Page Numbers window" />

**Page Numbers**: "1", "Page 1", "1 / 10" or "Page 1 of 10", in one of six positions, starting at any number.

## Scans

- **Searchable PDF** adds an invisible text layer to scanned pages, so you can search and copy.
- **Copy Text (OCR)** puts the text of the whole PDF on the clipboard.
- **Compress** makes big PDFs (often full of photos) much smaller.
