Compress works on photos, video, audio and PDF, one file or many at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag the file(s) and tap Ctrl">The tools wheel opens.</Step>
  <Step title="Drop on COMPRESS">The Compress window opens with the size before and after.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how small">
    Pick a preset (**High quality**, **Balanced**, **Smallest**), move the **Target size** slider, or type an
    **Exact size** in KB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Compress">The smaller copy is saved next to the original, named like `photo Compressed.jpg`.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="tool-compress" alt="The Compress window for a photo" caption="Target size, exact size and an optional resize. The estimate updates as you move the sliders." />

## Tips

- **Resize** shrinks the picture's dimensions as well, which saves the most for big photos.
- WAV and AIFF are turned into lossless FLAC: smaller, with no loss in quality.
- Need it for one place? The notch's [Fit WhatsApp, Fit Email and Fit Web](../notch-drop/) zones choose the
  settings for you.
- The notch's Jobs tab shows how much was saved when it's done.
