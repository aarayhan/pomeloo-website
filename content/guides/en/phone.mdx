Send to Phone works with any phone that has a camera and a browser. The files travel over your own Wi-Fi.

<Demo name="phone" />

## PC → phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the files">
    Drag them and tap <Keys k="Alt" /> → **Send to Phone**, or drop them on the notch's **Phone** zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code with the phone camera">The phone and the PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap a file on the phone to download it">The page on the phone lists every file you sent.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="notch-phone" alt="The Phone tab in the notch with a QR code" caption="The QR code shows right in the notch." />

## Phone → PC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the QR code">Tray icon → **Receive from phone**, or the notch's **Phone** tab.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan it, then choose files on the phone">Photos, videos, documents: anything the phone can share.</Step>
  <Step title="Find them in Downloads\PomeloO">The notch tells you when files arrive.</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- The link is random and only works while the QR code is open. Close it (or press **Stop**) and the transfer ends.
- Nothing goes through the internet or a cloud service.
- The first time, Windows may ask whether PomeloO may use the network: allow it on **Private networks**.
- Doesn't load on the phone? Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network, and the phone not on
  mobile data).
