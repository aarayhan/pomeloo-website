<Keys k="Shift" />.

## Trim

<Screenshot src="tool-trimVideo" alt="The Trim Video window with a player and In/Out marks" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Play or scrub to where the part you want starts">Then click **Set In**.</Step>
  <Step title="Move to where it should end">Then click **Set Out**.</Step>
  <Step title="Press Trim">The cut is frame-accurate and saved as a new file.</Step>
</Steps>

## Split

<Screenshot src="tool-splitVideo" alt="The Split Video window" />

Cut one video into parts: at points you choose, into equal parts, or every N seconds or minutes. Choose exact cuts,
or fast cuts on keyframes (no re-encoding, much quicker).

## And the rest

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Compress** | A smaller file, to a percentage or exact size ([guide](../compress/)) |
| **Remove Audio** | The same video, silent |
| **Crop Video** | Keep a region of the picture |
| **Change Video Speed** | Faster or slower |
| **Video Snapshots** | Exact frames, or N evenly spaced frames, as full-resolution PNG |
| **Redact Video** | Blur or cover regions, each with its own time range |
| **Burn In Subtitles** | Draws an .srt, .vtt or .ass into the picture; a subtitle file with the video's name is picked up automatically |
| **Metadata** | View streams, chapters and tags; edit or strip them |
| **Join Videos** | Select several clips: reorder them, get one MP4 |
